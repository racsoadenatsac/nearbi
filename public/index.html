The data rejected our own hypothesis. Here's what it found instead.

We bet that NEAR Intents volume leads real NEAR usage better than the NEAR price. It doesn't: 7 of 144 lead tests clear the 95% noise band, about the 7.2 chance alone would produce. Instead, Intents and NEAR's own users are moving in opposite directions: Intents cross-chain movements grew 521.4× while real users outside one app fell 92.3%. And "real users" turned out to be dominated by that one app, KaiChing: 82.9% of all real-user account-weeks.
